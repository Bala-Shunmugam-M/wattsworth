Add statistical rigor: OLS diagnostics, out-of-sample CV(RMSE), autocorrelation-corrected M&V

Addresses the #2 consensus finding from the agent audits: the baseline reported
only in-sample fit and the savings significance test assumed i.i.d. daily data.

baseline.py:
- BaselineModel now carries cv_rmse_oos (chronological 80/20 hold-out),
  durbin_watson, max_vif, and bp_pvalue (Breusch-Pagan).
- fit_baseline computes these; validate_oos flag controls the hold-out.

mv.py:
- summarize_savings replaces the naive i.i.d. t-test with an
  autocorrelation-corrected one: effective N = n*(1-rho)/(1+rho) from lag-1
  autocorrelation, tested against t with n_eff-1 df. Reports lag1_autocorr and
  n_effective.

UI:
- Page 1: "Regression diagnostics" panel (OOS CV(RMSE), Durbin-Watson, max VIF,
  Breusch-Pagan p).
- Page 2: significance verdict now shows the autocorrelation correction (rho,
  effective N).

Tests: 67 passing (+6: diagnostics populated, OOS computed, OOS disable flag,
autocorrelation reported, n_eff shrinks under strong autocorrelation).

On the clean demo baseline: in-sample CV(RMSE) 2.6% vs out-of-sample 4.2%,
DW 1.95, max VIF 1.0 — the honest in-vs-out gap is now visible.
